Guide to a Healthy Diet

A balanced diet typically consists of the right proportions of carbohydrates, proteins,
fats, vitamins, minerals, and water. Carbohydrates should generally make up 45-65% of
daily caloric intake, proteins 10-35%, and fats 20-35%, according to general dietary
guidelines.

Whole grains, such as brown rice, oats, and whole wheat, are preferred over refined
grains because they retain more fiber and nutrients. Fiber aids digestion and helps
maintain stable blood sugar levels.

Protein sources include both animal-based options like chicken, fish, and eggs, and
plant-based options like lentils, chickpeas, tofu, and beans. Plant-based proteins are
often lower in saturated fat and contain beneficial fiber.

Adults are generally recommended to drink around 2 to 3 liters of water per day,
though this varies based on body weight, activity level, and climate.

Added sugars should be limited to less than 10% of total daily calories, according to
World Health Organization guidelines, as excessive sugar intake is linked to obesity,
type 2 diabetes, and heart disease.

Regular meals, portion control, and minimizing ultra-processed foods are commonly
recommended practices for maintaining long-term metabolic health.
