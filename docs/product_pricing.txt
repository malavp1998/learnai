CloudSync Pricing Plans

CloudSync offers three subscription tiers: Starter, Pro, and Enterprise.

The Starter plan costs ₹499 per month and includes 50GB of storage, support for up to
3 connected devices, and standard email support with a 48-hour response time.

The Pro plan costs ₹1,499 per month and includes 500GB of storage, support for up to 10
connected devices, priority email support with a 12-hour response time, and access to
version history for the last 30 days.

The Enterprise plan is custom-priced based on team size, starting at ₹4,999 per month
for teams of up to 25 users. It includes unlimited storage, unlimited devices, a dedicated
account manager, 24/7 phone support, single sign-on (SSO), and unlimited version history.

All plans come with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. Annual billing gives a
20% discount compared to monthly billing on all plans. Refunds are available within 30
days of purchase for annual plans only; monthly plans are non-refundable but can be
cancelled anytime.
