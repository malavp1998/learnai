Acme Corp Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month and can be carried forward up to a
maximum of 10 unused days into the next calendar year. Any leave beyond 10 days that
remains unused by December 31st will be forfeited.

To request leave, employees must submit a request through the HR portal at least 3
working days in advance for planned leave. Sick leave does not require advance notice
but must be reported to your manager before 10 AM on the day of absence.

New employees are eligible for paid leave only after completing 90 days of employment.
During the probation period, employees may take unpaid leave with manager approval.

Maternity leave is 26 weeks paid, and paternity leave is 2 weeks paid, in accordance with
company policy. Bereavement leave of up to 5 days is granted for the death of an immediate
family member.

Public holidays observed by the company are separate from annual leave and are published
in the company calendar at the start of each year.
